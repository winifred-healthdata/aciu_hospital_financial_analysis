# Business Questions

## Project Objective

The objective of this analysis is to evaluate the financial performance of ACIU Hospital using revenue and expense data from the hospital's monthly financial reports.

The analysis focuses on understanding the hospital's revenue sources, operating expenses, and overall financial performance.

## Key Business Questions

### Revenue

1. What are the major sources of hospital revenue?

2. What percentage of total revenue does each revenue source contribute?

3. How does revenue change over time?

### Expenses

4. What are the major expense categories?

5. What is the hospital's total operating cost?

6. How do different expense categories contribute to total expenses?

### Financial Performance

7. What is the hospital's operating result after accounting for expenses?

8. How does the hospital's financial performance change over time?

9. Which revenue and expense categories have the greatest impact on the hospital's financial performance?

## Purpose of the Analysis

These questions guided the data preparation, data modelling, calculations, and dashboard development carried out in Power BI.

The goal is to transform the hospital's financial data into meaningful insights that can support financial monitoring and decision-making.
